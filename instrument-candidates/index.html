About the sounds & credits +

These are candidate sound designs inspired by familiar eighties timbres. The synths, drums, flute and orchestra hit are synthesized here; they are not clips from the referenced records or exact hardware emulations. Reference names are listening directions, not claims of matching the original patch.

The voice slots use actual CC0 recordings: EllenMentor’s layered female vocal, Cough-E’s robot voice, Kenney’s spoken “ready” and “go,” and DJ N.D.5 / leonschi13’s “Yeah.” The two choir voices are different edits and treatments of the same source recording. Freesound assets use their high-quality MP3 previews.

Group playback is an original four-bar audition groove. Every bank voice appears over one cycle; the optional common backing beat helps compare the sets. Switch it off to hear only the sixteen selected voices. These studies do not change either Bongo Planet application.
