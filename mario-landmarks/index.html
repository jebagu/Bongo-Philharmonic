What “fits” means here

The current landmark is small and floats just above the pole. Tall, simple shapes should keep their identity as the planet turns. A castle tower could work beautifully; an entire castle is likely to crowd the instruments.

What happens after choosing one?
  • Inspect the actual downloadable archive and convert its supported format to GLB if needed.
  • Check separate meshes, texture paths, materials, orientation and scale.
  • Extract a turret for whole-castle options; simplify detail that disappears at landmark size.
  • Test the silhouette and any flag animation in the Mario Paint view.

Real models, linked to their makers

Previews and interactive models are served by Sketchfab. Triangle counts, download availability and the exact upload-license labels were checked through its public model API on 16 October 2026. Downloads may require a Sketchfab account.

Uploader licenses do not establish rights to Nintendo designs or extracted game assets. This is a local reference gallery; no archives have been downloaded or models integrated.
