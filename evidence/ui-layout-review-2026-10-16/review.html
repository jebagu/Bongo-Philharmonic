One consistent frame around every workspace

Keep the existing visual style and the useful controls. Stabilize the header and bottom playback bar, then let each workspace use the center for its own task.

Recommended organization

HeaderSame brand, workspace tabs, song title, Files and settings access in every workspace. Keep their order and positions stable. Performance stays an ordinary selected tab; remove the separate Exit Performance requirement.
Playback barAlways full width at the bottom, below every sidebar. Stable left-to-right order: Play/Pause, Stop, Loop, position; then BPM and master volume. Keep Play’s size fixed when its label becomes Pause. Show the current song when browsing Library or FAQ.
Work areaPlacement: instruments left, sphere right. Composer: compact instrument strip above the large score, with editing tools beside the score. Performance: large sphere and compact score. Library and FAQ: scrolling content. Shared controls stay outside the scrolling area.
Sphere controlsVisual style, Reset view and Slow rotate in the same sphere corner in Placement and Performance. These do not replace navigation. Appearance changes remain separate from sound-set changes.
Song and selection settingsFiles owns save/load/import/export/recovery. Song settings owns meter and score length. Loop-range access sits beside Loop. Note and performer properties stay contextual. Undo/Redo need one stable location and disabled states rather than shifting other controls.
Playback behaviorSwitching workspaces and browsing the library preserve play/pause, position, tempo, volume and loop. Opening another composition is explicit and loads paused. A musical edit retains the current pause-to-edit behavior. Loop remains one toggle and Stop remains stop-and-rewind.

Decision points

Play locationRecommend the fixed lower-left bottom bar. Placing Play left of the treble clef is a coherent alternative only if that same control position is reserved in Placement and Library, which have no visible staff. Do not add a second Play control.
Performance score heightThe persistent bottom bar takes some height. Keep the score above it and let the sphere use the remaining space; compare dense scores before shrinking the notation. An adjustable score divider is a possible later refinement, not a prerequisite.
Selection toolsA compact reserved row avoids vertical movement when selecting a note, but costs height even when nothing is selected. A side inspector keeps score height, but costs width. Start with a shorter palette and compare before introducing a sidebar.
Meaning of TrackThe current top tabs are Placement, Composer, Performance, Library and FAQ. Track has not been identified as a current screen. Pending clarification, song-wide settings are treated as Song settings, while performer-specific controls remain contextual.
Narrow screensUse one consistent compact header and bottom-control arrangement at a given screen size. Overflow lower-priority settings before allowing Play/Stop/Loop to move or vanish. This run inspected the default desktop viewport; narrow-screen acceptance remains unverified.

What should be checked before accepting a future change

At the same viewport size, Play, Stop and Loop should have the same screen position in all five workspaces. Switching views should preserve playback and score context. The longest labels and dense scores should fit; scrolling content should not move the header or playback controls.

Keep visible keyboard focus, meaningful labels and adequate click targets. Note and performer controls should be reachable without hiding the music. The small note Deselect control and compact score buttons warrant a target-size check. This review is not a full keyboard, screen-reader or contrast audit.

Step 1

Placement — empty band

Needs alignment

The header is stable and the palette is clear. The bottom bar begins after the sidebar, so Play sits at x=335 rather than the x=31 used in Composer.

Recommendation: Use a full-width bottom bar outside the work area. Keep instrument choices and performer details in the left panel.

Step 2

Composer — empty score

Usable; crowded vertically

The normal header remains, but the instrument area is tall. The score receives much less height than the palette and staff can extend beyond its visible area.

Recommendation: Shorten the palette heading and combine selection information into a compact strip; give the score the recovered space.

Step 3

Performance — empty score

Strong presentation; inconsistent controls

The workspace tabs, title, Files and More disappear. Visual style, camera and playback replace them. Play moves to the top right.

Recommendation: Retain the regular header and bottom bar. Keep the sphere and compact read-only score in the center.

Step 4

Library

Mostly consistent

The ordinary header and full-width bottom playback bar remain. The current song is Untitled while library rows offer other songs through Open.

Recommendation: Keep the structure and explicitly identify the current song in the playback area. Browsing and filtering should preserve playback; opening another song should load it paused.

Step 5

Composer — Pachelbel’s Canon

Needs more score space

The first passage contains bass notes but they are initially below the visible portion of the score. The top instrument panel takes about 193 pixels at this viewport.

Recommendation: Reduce the instrument panel height and retain section/passage navigation beside the score. Consider opening the score at the relevant register as a separate usability refinement.

Step 6

More / song settings

Needs clearer grouping

The dialog combines duplicate file actions, title, Theme, Meter, score operations and loop-range settings. Its lower controls require scrolling.

Recommendation: Keep saving/loading in Files; group appearance together; keep Meter and score length in Song settings. Put loop-range access beside Loop and note operations near the selection.

Step 7

Files / Save & share

Clear on its own

Files provides saving, loading, import, export and recovery with explanatory copy. The same core actions are also present in More.

Recommendation: Make Files the consistent home for these actions. Retain the useful explanation and recovery behavior.

Step 8

FAQ

Mostly consistent

The regular header and bottom transport remain while help scrolls in the center.

Recommendation: Keep this structure, showing which current song the persistent playback controls operate on.

Step 9

Composer — selected note

Useful controls; reduces score height

Pitch, duration, performer, note volume and actions appear as an extra row, reducing the score viewport. The volume label clearly distinguishes note volume from master volume.

Recommendation: Give selection tools a predictable location and keep master controls in the fixed bottom bar. Choose between a reserved compact row and a side inspector only after comparing available score space.

Step 10

Placement — selected performer

Useful tools; details can be buried

The selected performer appears below the complete instrument palette. Its location and move actions lie below the visible sidebar area at this viewport. Play is at (335,650).

Recommendation: Scroll the palette independently or provide a compact expandable selected-performer area so the current selection remains easy to reach.

Step 11

Performance — Pachelbel’s Canon

Keep the presentation; replace the surrounding controls

The sphere, instruments, Mario and compact score work together well. The top bar is still entirely different and omits BPM and master volume. Play is at approximately (801,30).

Recommendation: Use the same header, title and bottom transport as the other workspaces. Place Visual style, Reset view and Slow rotate in a consistent corner of the sphere in Placement and Performance.

Evidence limits

The review used a separate in-app browser tab and opened a library example. An unintended test note during a hit-test was immediately undone; the selected-note screenshot shows the restored score. No deliberate song, appearance or playback-setting edits were made. No audio playback, narrow-screen checks or production-site checks were performed.

Suggested next step: choose the permanent Play location, then compare Composer and Performance mockups with the same header and footer.
