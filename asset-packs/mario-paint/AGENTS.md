# Bongo Philharmonic — local Mario assets

This is this project's own copy of the first original Mario Paint sprite/model/native-note pack. Keep original pixels, models and prepared-note envelopes. Runtime adapters are in `foundation/src/asset-packs/mario-paint/`. Read README.md for the original source/provenance and tuning exceptions; its old Planet URLs/tool paths are historical. No input ROM is copied here. Assets have no established redistribution license; keep them local and out of future public payloads. No restore of deleted alternate/upscaled assets is authorized.
